## What the site is

The site publishes Paul's articles and books, and a set of applets you can use in your browser. The articles, books and applets are Paul's unless an article says otherwise, and you may read, use and link to them freely. Copying them elsewhere needs his permission, except where an article or its source code carries a licence that says otherwise.

## Your account

An account is for one person, aged 13 or over. You are responsible for what happens through the ways you sign in, so keep your email account and passkeys secure. You can delete your account at any time on the Account page.

Your display name and picture appear beside your comments. Don't use a name or picture that pretends to be someone else, that you have no right to use, or that is unlawful.

## Comments

Every comment, and every edit to one, is read by a moderator before it appears, and the site may decline any of them. That isn't a judgement of you; it keeps the discussion on topic and within the law. A comment you post stays yours. You let the site store it, show it with your display name beside the article it belongs to, and keep it in backups, for as long as it is published or until you withdraw it.

Don't post anything that is unlawful in Singapore, or that:

- harasses, threatens, bullies or stalks anyone, or encourages others to;
- reveals someone's personal details, such as where they live, without their consent;
- shares intimate images, or any sexual content involving a child;
- says something false about a real person or organisation that could harm their reputation;
- you have no right to share, such as someone else's work copied without permission;
- pretends to be someone else, or is spam or advertising.

You can edit a comment, which a moderator reads again, or withdraw it, which erases its text at once. A moderator may remove a published comment, and may suspend an account that breaks these terms. Where someone has replied to a comment that is withdrawn or removed, a note says so in its place.

## Reporting a comment

Under any approved comment, Report sends it to the moderators with your reason. You can also write to [paul@parkscomputing.com](mailto:paul@parkscomputing.com), including if you are not a member, for example if a comment harms you or reveals your personal details. Reports are looked at within seven days, and sooner for intimate images, threats or someone's personal details. A report doesn't remove a comment by itself; a moderator decides.

The site complies with lawful directions and orders about content on it, including those of Singapore's Online Safety Commission, under the Protection from Online Falsehoods and Manipulation Act, and of the courts.

Please don't use the site in a way that harms it or other people. That means no attacks on the server, no attempts to get around its limits or into other people's accounts, and no automated use heavy enough to burden it. The site may limit or close an account that does these things.
