Sight Reader fingerboards: mockups

Drawn with the drawing code the applet would use. The names are the written pitches, so a guitar string that sounds an octave lower is named at the pitch on the page.

Guitar, labels on the C notes and open strings (the default)

The low E string is at the bottom, the high E at the top, the nut at the left, as in tablature. The column left of the nut is the open string. Green is a note named; here a G has been named, and it is lit everywhere it lies on the neck: the open third string and frets 5, 10 and 15 of the three strings below.

Guitar, every note labelled, sharps and flats not named

With Name sharps and flats off the black-key positions are dimmed and cannot be pressed, as the piano keeps its black keys off. One wrong press shows red.

Violin, labels on the C notes and open strings

The violin has no frets, so there are only faint lines at each semitone and the finger dot sits on the line. Four strings, G at the bottom to E at the top, the nut at the left.

Violin, every note labelled

The same board with each position named, to show how it reads at the full width of the page.

Open strings (fret 0) sit in the column left of the nut and can be pressed like any other position. A click on any position that has the right pitch is right, as a click on the right piano key is right.
